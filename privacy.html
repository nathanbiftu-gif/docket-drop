Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

Docket Drop collects the minimum information needed to run a newsletter business. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

1. What we collect

  • Email address — when you subscribe (free or Pro) through our signup forms.
  • Subscription details — whether you're on free or Pro, and your billing period.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe through our checkout provider. We never see or store your card number.
  • Basic site data — our host (GitHub Pages) logs routine technical data like IP addresses to serve the site. Our email embeds may set cookies to make forms work.

2. How we use it

To send you the newsletter, manage your Pro access, send service emails (receipts, subscription notices, material policy changes), and comply with the law. That's it — we don't build advertising profiles and we don't sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

3. Who we share it with

  • Kit — delivers our emails and hosts signup forms and checkout.
  • Stripe — processes Pro payments.

We share data with these providers only so the service works. We don't share it with anyone else except when the law requires it.

4. Cookies

We don't run our own tracking cookies. Embedded signup and checkout forms from our providers may set cookies to function; their policies govern those.

5. Your rights

  • Unsubscribe anytime — every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Ask what we have / ask us to delete it — reply to any Docket Drop email and we'll handle it.
  • California residents — under the CCPA you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of any "sale" of personal information. We don't sell personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while you're subscribed and for a reasonable period afterward for records and legal compliance, then delete or anonymize it.

7. Children

Docket Drop is written for business owners and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, and email is never fully private — keep that in mind with anything you send us.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll post updates here with a new effective date and email subscribers about material changes.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests? Reply to any Docket Drop email.
